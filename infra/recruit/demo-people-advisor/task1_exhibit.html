Exit-interview extracts

12 of 35 Q2 leavers completed an exit interview. Extracts below are a non-random sample of the free-text record.

“The job itself was fine. What wore me down was the roster. If you were in favour you got the weekends you asked for; if you weren’t, you didn’t. Everyone on nights knows exactly what I mean.”

Customer service agent · Customer Operations (night desk) · 2 yrs

“I asked twice to come off the night rotation. Both times my shifts got worse the month after. You learn to stop asking.”

Senior agent · Customer Operations · 3 yrs

“Pay, mostly. I can get fifteen per cent more elsewhere doing the same job.”

Analyst · Supply Chain & Logistics · 1 yr

“Genuinely sad to go — brilliant team. We’re relocating for my partner’s work, that’s all.”

Coordinator · Marketing & Digital · 4 yrs

“Weekend and holiday allocation on the night desk depends on who the team lead likes that month. The survey never asks about that.”

Agent · Customer Operations (night desk) · 18 months

“Learned a lot in three years. Nights are hard, and lately harder. I’d come back to a day role.”

Agent · Customer Operations · 3 yrs

Methodology & notes

  • The engagement index is a composite of six survey items (role clarity, workload, manager support, fairness, confidence in leadership, advocacy), scored 0–10.
  • Function scores are simple means of respondents in that function; the group figure is the headcount-weighted mean across functions.
  • Participation varies by function and working pattern; scores for lower-participation groups carry wider uncertainty.
  • Exit-interview extracts are drawn from a voluntary, non-random sample (12 of 35 Q2 leavers) and are not weighted.
  • Team-level, shift-level and roster-level cuts of the survey and leaver data are available on request via the Halcyon Knowledge System.
